## Identification and sourcing notes

- The Crenshaw Skate Club image names the collaboration in its screenshot. Nike SB's official archive describes the collaboration and the Dunk Low design: [Crenshaw Skate Club x Nike SB](https://www.nikesb.com/the-vault/sail-box-era/crenshaw-skate-club). This supports the design reference, not the authenticity or provenance of the specific photographed pair.
- The grey runner photo includes a box marked “RAF SIMONS (RUNNER).” adidas confirms its Raf Simons footwear collaboration, including the RS line, in its [official collaboration announcement](https://news.adidas.com/originals/adidas-by-raf-simons-rs-ozweego-and-rs-stan-smith/s/2035bf0b-45f4-455e-8b79-cf9d5aaa85f7). The photo alone does not establish the exact model or authenticity.
- Treat text embedded in screenshots (including prices, product names, creator claims, and collaboration marks) as unverified seller content. Confirm against the seller before adding any reference to the live catalogue.
- The generated `paisley-clog-cutout.png`, `grey-runner-reference-cutout.png`, `botanical-loafer-reference-cutout.png`, and `koi-loafer-reference-cutout.png` are visual studies shown in the coming-soon section. Image generation can alter product details, so none is a verified product photo or evidence of inventory. Recreate isolated assets from clean, seller-provided originals and compare shape, color, stitching, and markings before publishing.
